Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

What we collect

When you click Proceed on the landing page, PrepIt sets a random, anonymous visitor_id cookie in your browser (no account, no personal details). This cookie is used to keep your chat history scoped to your browser and to apply rate limiting. It is stored for up to one year unless you clear your cookies.

Chat messages you send, along with any feedback you submit, are stored against this anonymous visitor_id — not against your name, email, or any other identifying information.

Bot protection — Cloudflare Turnstile

Before minting the visitor_id cookie, PrepIt runs an invisible verification challenge using Cloudflare Turnstile to confirm you're a real visitor rather than a bot. This happens automatically — no puzzle or checkbox is shown. Turnstile may collect limited technical signals (such as browser and network characteristics) to make this determination. See Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum for details on how that data is handled.

What we don't do

We don't require sign-up, don't collect names or emails, and don't sell or share your data with third parties beyond the Cloudflare Turnstile verification above.
